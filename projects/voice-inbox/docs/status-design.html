Waiting — four substates

SubstateWhat it means
recordedCapture done, uploading.
transcribingAudio landed, transcript being made.
transcribedTranscript ready, being placed into a conversation.
routedAssigned to a topic and worker.

Transcribed may jump straight to Running when placement and pickup happen in one step. A placement that cannot find its conversation shows the existing "not placed" entry. A transcription failure keeps today's "Couldn't hear that" wording and re-record button.

Running — visible substates

SubstateWhat it means
startedFirst progress signal.
planningReading and diagnosing.
buildingEdits and commands.
verifyingTests and review.
needs youA question waiting on you (drives the Action needed block).

Workers signal planning / building / verifying by starting their progress line with a small phase tag. Untagged progress shows as plain "Running". No update for a while shows through the last-updated time chip LIVE today, never a sentence.
