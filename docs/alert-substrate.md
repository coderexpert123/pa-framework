# Alert substrate rules (extracted from the root brain 2026-09-03, budget-pressure doctrine)

Active pointer: root `CLAUDE.md` § Standards & Conventions links here. Read before touching alert families, dedup keys, notify escalation, or any watchdog's paging behavior.

- **Alert substrate rules (2026-08-23, after a 548-alert week — internal review; full history is private)**: one failure produces ONE alert family (an intermediate failover hop never pages `Worker exited`; a parked skill is not also reported stale; a lock-busy skill run is `Skill skipped (lock busy)`, not `Skill failed`); dedup keys never embed PIDs, topic ids or counts (`bg-leak` was 88 un-dedupable pages about conhost/tee wrappers — now a log line, `bg-orphan` is the real-leak detector); an UNCHANGED alert body escalates its dedup window 1 h → 2 h → … → 24 h (`notify.ts`, `escalate:false` to opt out) and a timed-out send writes a 10-min mute so it cannot feed the 429 loop; `staleness-check` is THE dead-man's switch (transition-keyed `staleness:<hash>`, parked-aware, `cost_tier: off_peak`-aware; `skill-cadence-audit` retired); maintenance jobs that fail retry on a ladder 0/30m/2h/8h/24h (`MAINTENANCE_FAILURE_BACKOFF_MS`, `lastAttemptAt` in the ledger), never every tick; the daily `alert-census` job writes `~/.pa/alert-census.json` and pages only above `PA_ALERT_CENSUS_NOTIFY_PER_DAY` (50). Operator-action conditions (Google OAuth `invalid_grant`) route to the general topic via `pa/scripts/google_reauth_kick.py` (12 h sessions, 6 h send rate-limit, `/reauth` bot command) and age-escalate in `human-gated-blocker-watch`, never to pa-alerts.
- **Loop and ledger liveness families (2026-09-16)**: `catchup-loop-stalled` is paged by the per-minute catchup launcher, through a fresh `pa notify`, whenever it restarts a stuck or stalled loop. Its body names only the cause, so a repeating cause escalates instead of paging per restart (`docs/catchup-watchdog.md`). `catchup-loop-pid-reused` is paged once at warn severity, through the same launcher, when the loop's PID file names another process: the loop died uncleanly, and nothing is killed. `maintenance-freshness:<hash>` is paged by `staleness-check` when a declared job of either host has no ledger attempt or skip for 15 minutes; the key is transition-keyed on the sorted stale set.
